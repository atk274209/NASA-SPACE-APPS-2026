## Coverage gaps

- **Fluid shifts / hydration**: no dedicated HRP risk page exists for this topic. Coverage comes from the Cardiovascular and Vision Laboratory page, the SANS and cardiovascular risk pages, and a short 2014 NTRS presentation abstract. Hydration appears only briefly, in the nutrition risk page (ground data on dehydration). No primary hydration-monitoring source was found.
- **Muscle atrophy (mechanistic detail)**: covered only through the combined HRP cardiorespiratory/musculoskeletal fitness risk page. No muscle-specific evidence report or primary dataset was read.
- **Radiation (non-cancer)**: only the carcinogenesis risk page was read. CNS and cardiovascular radiation effects are covered indirectly in the behavioral-health and cardiovascular risk pages.
- **Full evidence reports**: no HRP evidence-report PDF was read in full. The bone report is represented by its NTRS abstract only.
- **Exercise countermeasure hardware (ARED) specifications**: mentioned only within the bone and fitness risk pages. No dedicated ARED source was read.
